## Reading the checklist

Severity is residual impact on the named audience: **blocks** prevents the advertised alpha install/core launch or violates approval safety; **degrades** limits a feature or has a workaround; **cosmetic** affects guidance/presentation. An unsupported platform requirement can block its users without becoming a promised alpha feature. Network homes, unusual WSL layouts and alternative service environments are unverified risks, not reproduced blockers.
Decisions use exactly **configurable** (implemented owner setting, owning issue given), **detected** (implemented observation/refusal, remaining diagnostic work named), **documented requirement** (supported constraint), **deferred issue** (work needed). F-names are proposals in the follow-up section, not implemented settings. Report 01 platform scope, report 11 isolation/local-state policy, report 19 optional clients, and decision records for #268, #443, #446, #476 and #50 take precedence over older audit wording.
Evidence is repository-relative `file:line`; S2/S3 are reproducible absence searches below. Source inspection is not a runtime certification. IDs retain the coverage spine's numbering so dropped/merged entries do not silently renumber remaining assumptions.
