# Stops wait in a lane of their own: two workers, two waiting places

Issue #217. Decided by Theo (option B in the issue); the sizes and the routing of each
command by the implementing session.

- **`session-end`, `session-sleep` and `resource-stop` run in the stop lane.** They only stop
  an agent or an execution, and a stop may hold its worker through the grace and kill wait
  (up to 10 s for a session, 20 s for an execution). In the bulk lane they waited behind launches and were answered `busy` when it
  was full; in the control lane eight slow stops would have taken input and status away.
- **`session-restart` and `session-restart-all` stay in the bulk lane.** They stop and then
  launch, and a launch is bulk work.
- **Two workers, two waiting.** Two stops run at once, so one agent that ignores `SIGTERM`
  does not block the next stop. The waiting places are few on purpose: the last session stop
  admitted is answered within 20 s, inside the TUI's 30 s request deadline. A deeper queue
  would let a client time out while the daemon still runs its stop; a full stop lane answers
  `busy` at once, naming it, like the other lanes.
- **Rejected:** stops in the control lane (option A), because a slow stop would hold a
  control worker for the whole escalation; keeping stops in bulk and amending report 03
  (option C), because report 15 scenario 3 requires stops served under load.

Evidence: `crates/cockpit-daemon/tests/stop_lane.rs`.
