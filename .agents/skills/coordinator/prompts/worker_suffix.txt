One /code-review at medium on the branch delta, focused tests pass, PR to main, arm gh pr merge --auto --squash. Do not end your turn while waiting on long checks or CI: wait with a blocking loop (Monitor until-loop) so you wake on the result. After arming auto-merge, wait for the merge or a failed check with this Monitor until-loop (N is the PR number): `until gh pr view N --json state --jq .state | grep -qE 'MERGED|CLOSED' || gh pr checks N --json name,bucket --jq 'any(.[]; .bucket=="fail" and .name!="greenfield-macos")' | grep -qx true; do sleep 30; done; gh pr view N --json state`. Ending the turn after arming auto-merge leaves a CI failure unseen until someone nudges you. The one exception: a /code-review skill or a command you started with run_in_background wakes you when it finishes, so for those alone you may end the turn. Keep large outputs (build dirs, measurements) under your clone or your session temp folder, never /tmp. Ignore the greenfield-macos check: macOS is not supported yet. Never touch the installed Torlek's tmux server (no tmux -L, nothing under /tmp/tmux-<uid>): use fakes or tmux -S under your clone.
